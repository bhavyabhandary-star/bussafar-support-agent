BusSafar Help Docs
BusSafar is a made-up business. This document is practice data for a learning project.
BusSafar is a made-up app for booking intercity bus tickets with many bus operators across India.
Frequently asked questions
Q: How do I book a bus ticket?
A: Search your route and date, pick a bus and seat, add passenger details and pay.
Q: Do I need to print my ticket?
A: No. Show the m-ticket SMS or the ticket in the app, with a photo ID.
Q: Which ID proof is needed?
A: Any government photo ID: Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, voter ID or passport.
Q: Can I book for someone else?
A: Yes. Enter the traveller's name and age. They must carry their own ID.
Q: How early should I reach the boarding point?
A: At least 15 minutes before departure.
Q: Which payment methods are accepted?
A: UPI, cards, net banking and wallets. Cash is not accepted.
Q: How do I cancel my ticket?
A: Go to My Trips, choose the booking and tap Cancel. The refund depends on how early you cancel.
Q: How much refund will I get if I cancel?
A: More than 24 hours before departure: 90%. 12 to 24 hours before: 50%. Less than 12 hours: no refund.
Q: When will I get my refund?
A: Within 5 to 7 working days, to the original payment method.
Q: Can I change my travel date?
A: Yes, once, up to 24 hours before departure, for a ₹100 fee plus any fare difference.
Q: Can I change the passenger name?
A: No. Cancel the ticket and book a new one.
Q: What if the operator cancels the bus?
A: You get a full refund automatically within 3 working days.
Q: What if the bus is late?
A: Track the bus live in the app. If it is more than 3 hours late, you can cancel for a full refund.
Q: How much luggage can I carry?
A: One bag up to 15 kg and one small handbag. The operator may charge for extra luggage.
Q: Are pets allowed?
A: Pets are not allowed on most buses. Check the bus details page.
Q: Can I choose my seat?
A: Yes, from the seat map while booking. Seats for women are marked in pink.
Q: Do you have sleeper buses?
A: Yes. Filter by Sleeper or AC Sleeper while searching.
Q: Do you have discounts?
A: First-time users get ₹100 off with code FIRSTSAFAR. One coupon per booking.
Q: Is there a safety feature for women?
A: Yes. Share your live trip with family from the app and use the SOS button in an emergency.
Q: I didn't get my ticket SMS.
A: Open My Trips in the app. You can resend the ticket by SMS or email from there.
Policies
Policy: Cancellation and refund policy
More than 24 hours before departure: 90% refund.
12 to 24 hours before departure: 50% refund.
Less than 12 hours before departure: no refund.
Refunds reach the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
One date change is allowed up to 24 hours before departure, for ₹100 plus any fare difference.
Policy: Travel rules
Every traveller must carry a government photo ID.
Reach the boarding point 15 minutes before departure.
One bag up to 15 kg and one small handbag per traveller.
Smoking, alcohol and loud music are not allowed on the bus.
Policy: Delays and operator cancellations
If the operator cancels the bus, the full fare is refunded within 3 working days.
If the bus is more than 3 hours late, the traveller can cancel for a full refund.
In an emergency on the road, call 112 and use the SOS button in the app.
